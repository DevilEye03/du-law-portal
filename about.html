About Make Law Easy

Academic Initiative • Faculty of Law, University of Delhi

Make Law Easy (makelaweasy.in) is an editorial legal learning portal crafted specifically for law students of the University of Delhi (Faculty of Law), encompassing Campus Law Centre (CLC), Law Centre–I (LC-1), and Law Centre–II (LC-2).

Our Mission

Studying law at Delhi University is intellectually rigorous, grounded in extensive judicial case materials and evolving statutory jurisprudence. Make Law Easy was established to democratize top-tier academic resources by providing clear, structured, and exam-anchored study materials accessible to every law student free of charge.

What We Provide

Syllabus Lecture Notes

Exhaustive unit-by-unit breakdowns aligned directly with DU Faculty of Law course packs.

Landmark Case Briefs

Facts, issues, ratio decidendi, arguments, and exam tips for all prescribed precedents.

Solved DU PYQs

Past year examination questions answered with university-standard model solutions.

Rapid Revision Capsules

High-yield doctrine summaries, core statutory provisions, and memory mnemonics for last-minute cramming.

Editorial Standards

Every case brief, doctrinal note, and model answer on Make Law Easy is curated with academic rigor, incorporating recent judicial developments from the Supreme Court of India, new criminal laws (BNS, BNSS, BSA 2023), and corporate law amendments.
