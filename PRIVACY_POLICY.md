## 3. THE PRINCIPLE OF DATA MINIMIZATION (NO LOGIN OR SUBSCRIPTION)

3.1. **Strictly Open Public Academic Platform:**  
Unlike commercial platforms or paid ed-tech services, Make Law Easy operates on an uncompromising **data minimization** architecture.

3.2. **No User Accounts or Passwords:**  
We do not operate a user registration portal. You do not need to create a profile, generate a username, establish a password, or submit credentials to access our syllabus notes, case briefs, statutory bare acts, or past year questions.

3.3. **No Commercial Subscriptions or Financial Data Collection:**  
We do not charge subscription fees, sell digital products through the Website, or maintain payment gateways. Consequently, **we do not request, process, or store credit card numbers, debit card details, bank account credentials, UPI handles, or billing addresses** on this Website.

## 4. CATEGORIES OF INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We limit the collection of personal information to what is strictly necessary to maintain the Website’s security, deliver requested educational content, and communicate with students who initiate direct contact.

### 4.1. Information You Provide to Us Voluntarily:
When you voluntarily reach out to us via direct email (`ankur@makelaweasy.in`) or participate in our Telegram discussion group, you may choose to provide:
* **Contact Details:** Name, email address, or Telegram handle;
* **Inquiry Subject and Content:** Academic questions, syllabus feedback, typo/errata reports, or suggestions for study notes.

### 4.2. Local Storage Information (Stored Exclusively on Your Device):
To provide a smooth, personalized study experience without requiring an account, the Website uses your browser’s standard **HTML5 Local Storage**:
* **Display Preferences:** Dark Mode / Light Mode toggle setting (`du_law_dark_mode`);
* **Curriculum Navigation:** Currently selected semester (`du_law_selected_semester`);
* **Study Progress:** Locally checked topics, study checklist items, and saved bookmarks (`du_law_portal_bookmarks`).  
* **Strict Privacy Guarantee:** *All Local Storage data remains strictly stored on your own local device and browser. It is NEVER transmitted, uploaded, or synced to any remote database or server.*

### 4.3. Information Collected Automatically (Technical Telemetry):
When your browser accesses the Website, our web hosting infrastructure (Google Firebase Hosting) automatically records standard, non-sensitive technical logs necessary for network transmission and security:
* **Network & Device Identifiers:** Anonymized IP address, operating system, browser type and version, device category (desktop, mobile, tablet), and screen resolution;
* **Browsing Telemetry:** Date and time of request, pages viewed, referring URLs, HTTP response status codes, and volume of data transmitted.

## 9. COOKIES, TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES, AND SERVER ANALYTICS (GA4 & GOOGLE ADSENSE)

### 9.1. Strictly Essential & Preference Cookies
Because our Website does not feature user accounts, we do **not** use persistent authentication cookies. We use minimal functional cookies or local storage for:
* **Display Preferences:** Remembering your Dark/Light mode theme preference;
* **Network & Security:** Content Delivery Network (CDN) security cookies and firewall tokens (Firebase Hosting).

### 9.2. Google Analytics 4 (GA4)
We use Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: `G-8ZG7VS1G45`) to assess how law students engage with our study dossiers. GA4 collects aggregated, non-personally identifiable telemetry such as:
* Daily visitor counts and active concurrent readers;
* Broad geographical distribution (city/country level);
* Most visited subject pages, landmark case briefs, and PYQ solutions.

GA4 is configured with **IP Anonymization / Masking**. You can opt out of Google Analytics across all websites by installing the official [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

### 9.3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies
To help keep Make Law Easy free and accessible to all law students, we may display non-intrusive advertisements served by third-party advertising partners, including **Google LLC (Google AdSense)**.

* **Third-Party Cookies:** Google and its advertising partners use cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve relevant advertisements based on a user's prior visits to this website and other websites on the internet.
* **Non-Intrusive Academic Experience:** Advertisements are placed responsibly so as never to obstruct or interfere with study text, case briefs, or revision charts.
* **How to Opt Out of Personalized Advertising:**
  * You can customize or opt out of personalized Google advertising at any time by visiting **[Google My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/)**.
  * You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting **[AboutAds Choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/)** or the **[Network Advertising Initiative](https://optout.networkadvertising.org/)**.
