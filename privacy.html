CLAUSE 03

3. The Principle of Data Minimization (No Login or Subscription)

3.1. Open Public Platform: Unlike commercial platforms or subscription portals, Make Law Easy operates on an uncompromising data minimization architecture.

Zero Credential Architecture

We do not operate a user registration portal. You do not need to create an account, enter passwords, or submit credentials to read our syllabus notes, landmark case briefs, or PYQ dossiers.

3.3. No Financial Data Collection: We do not charge subscription fees, sell digital products through the Website, or maintain payment gateways. Consequently, we do not request, process, or store credit card numbers, debit card details, bank account credentials, UPI handles, or billing addresses.

CLAUSE 04

4. Categories of Information We Collect

We limit data collection to what is strictly necessary to maintain security, ensure responsive performance, and communicate with students who reach out directly.

4.1. Information You Provide Voluntarily:

When you voluntarily email us (ankur@makelaweasy.in) or engage in our Telegram community, you may provide:

  • Contact Details: Your name, email address, or Telegram handle;
  • Inquiry Content: Academic questions, syllabus feedback, notes suggestions, or errata reports.

4.2. Local Storage (Stored Exclusively on Your Device):

To provide a personalized study experience without requiring a login, Make Law Easy utilizes standard browser HTML5 Local Storage:

  • Display Settings: Dark / Light mode theme setting (du_law_dark_mode);
  • Curriculum Navigation: Currently selected semester (du_law_selected_semester);
  • Study Progress: Checked topic completion items and bookmarks (du_law_portal_bookmarks).

Privacy Guarantee

All Local Storage data is stored strictly on your own device and browser. It is NEVER uploaded, transmitted, or synchronized to any remote database.

4.3. Information Collected Automatically (Technical Telemetry):

When your browser accesses the Website, our hosting infrastructure (Google Firebase Hosting) automatically logs standard, non-sensitive technical details:

  • Network Telemetry: Anonymized IP address, operating system, browser type, device category (desktop/mobile), and screen resolution;
  • Request Logs: Timestamp of request, pages visited, referring URLs, HTTP status codes, and bytes transferred.

CLAUSE 05

5. Information We Explicitly DO NOT Collect

Make Law Easy explicitly refrains from harvesting or collecting:

  • Device Contacts: We never inspect or access your device address book;
  • Social Media Profiles: We do not deploy intrusive social login plugins that scrape your social networks;
  • Financial Credentials: We do not collect bank credentials, credit/debit card numbers, or UPI PINs;
  • Biometric Data: We do not collect fingerprints, facial recognition scans, or voiceprints;
  • Precise GPS Location: We do not track real-time GPS coordinates;
  • Data Brokering: We do not sell, rent, or trade your browsing records to commercial data brokers.

CLAUSE 09

9. Cookies, Tracking Technologies, and Server Analytics (GA4 & Google AdSense)

9.1. Essential & Preference Cookies:

Because the Website has no user accounts, we do not use persistent authentication cookies. We use minimal functional local storage to remember your Dark/Light mode preference and semester navigation.

9.2. Google Analytics 4 (GA4):

We use Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: G-8ZG7VS1G45) to understand aggregate student readership. GA4 collects non-personally identifiable telemetry such as total daily visitors, broad city/country distribution, and most read case briefs. GA4 is configured with IP Anonymization. You can opt out globally via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

9.3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies:

To help keep Make Law Easy freely available to all law students, we may display non-intrusive advertisements served by third-party advertising partners, including Google LLC (Google AdSense).

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve advertisements based on a user's prior visits to this website and other websites across the internet.
  • Advertisements are formatted responsibly to ensure they never obstruct notes, case briefs, or study charts.
  • How to Opt Out of Personalized Ads:
    • You can manage or opt out of personalized Google advertising at any time by visiting Google My Ad Center.
    • You can also opt out of third-party vendor advertising cookies by visiting AboutAds Choices or the Network Advertising Initiative.

CLAUSE 14

14. Your Legal Rights as a Data Subject

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, India) and global regulations (GDPR, CCPA), you possess the following rights:

  • Right to Access / Summary: The right to request a summary of any personal data we hold about you;
  • Right to Correction: The right to request the correction of inaccurate or outdated details;
  • Right to Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): The right to request the deletion of your personal communications;
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: The right to have grievances addressed by our Grievance Redressal Officer;
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We never restrict or deny access to our free study notes for exercising your statutory rights.
